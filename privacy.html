Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026 · applies to indiabaygame.com

In short

  • You can play offline without giving us anything. Your progress stays in your browser.
  • To play online we keep a username, your game progress and a sign-in ID. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your email address.
  • We don't show ads, we don't sell your data and we don't use tracking or analytics tools.
  • You can delete your online account yourself at any time from the 👤 Profile button in the game.

Who we are

India Bay is a free open-world browser game set in India, run by the India Bay team at indiabaygame.com. "We" in this policy means the India Bay team.

Services we use

These companies process data on our behalf, under their own privacy terms:

  • Supabase: accounts, usernames, cloud saves, reports and support messages. Privacy
  • Cloudflare: the online server that connects players. Privacy
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website files. Privacy
  • Google: "Sign in with Google" (optional) and the fonts. Privacy
  • cdnjs and jsDelivr: deliver the 3D engine and sign-in code to your browser.

Your data may be processed outside India by these providers.

Your browser's storage

The game saves your progress, settings and muted players in your browser's local storage. This isn't used for advertising or tracking. Clearing your browser data removes it. Online players can get their progress back by signing in again with Google.

How long we keep it

  • Account, username and cloud save: until you delete your account.
  • Reports and suggestions: until the issue is dealt with, and no longer than 12 months.
  • Support messages: up to 12 months after we reply.
  • Guest accounts we haven't seen for a long time may be removed to keep the game tidy.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the game, open 👤 Profile, then Delete my online account. This permanently removes your username, cloud save and the suggestions and reports you sent.
  • See or correct your data: you can change your username in 👤 Profile. For a copy of your data or any other request, contact us using the details below.
  • Play without an account: choose Play offline on the start screen.
  • Control chat: mute or report any player from Phone → 🌐 Online.

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to access, correct or erase your personal data, and to raise a grievance with us. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

Children

India Bay is a cartoon game for a general audience, but online play lets you chat with strangers. If you are under 18, please play online only with permission from a parent or guardian. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us information without your consent, contact us and we'll delete it.

Keeping it safe

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and database rules let each player read and change only their own save. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't share personal details in chat.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also announce it on the game's start screen.
