Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026 · applies to indiabaygame.com

In short

  • You can play offline without giving us anything. Your progress stays in your browser.
  • When you create a player we ask for a username, your name and your date of birth. Your username is public; your name and date of birth stay private.
  • To play online we also keep your game progress and a sign-in ID. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address.
  • The only ads are billboards inside the game world, chosen by where you are in the game, your age group and the look you picked for your player (male or female). We don't sell your data, and we don't use ad networks, third-party tracking or analytics tools.
  • We count how the game is used (for example minutes driving, voice chat used, billboards seen) in our own database, to run and improve the game.
  • Accounts nobody plays are removed: guest accounts after 14 days, Google accounts after 60 days.
  • You can delete your online account yourself at any time from the 👤 Profile button in the game.

Who we are

India Bay is a free open-world browser game set in India, run by the India Bay team at indiabaygame.com. "We" in this policy means the India Bay team.

Your browser's storage

The game saves your player (including your name and date of birth), progress, settings and muted players in your browser's local storage. This isn't used for advertising or tracking. Clearing your browser data removes it. Online players can get their progress back by signing in again with Google.

How long we keep it

  • Account, username and cloud save: until you delete your account.
  • Reports about players: up to 24 months, including after the reporter's account is deleted.
  • Suggestions: until they are dealt with, and no longer than 12 months.
  • Support messages: up to 12 months after we reply.
  • Accounts nobody plays: guest accounts are removed after 14 days without playing, and Google accounts after 60 days, with their cloud save, messages and BayGram photos. The game shows this in 👤 Profile.
  • Usage counts and ad numbers: added up per day, for up to 24 months.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: open 👤 Profile, then Delete account. This permanently removes your username, name, date of birth and cloud save. Reports you sent about other players are kept, without your account details, so we can keep the game safe.
  • See or correct your data: you can change your username, name and date of birth in 👤 Profile → Settings → Profile. For a copy of your data or any other request, contact us using the details below.
  • Play without an account: choose Play offline on the start screen.
  • Control chat: mute or report any player from Phone → 🌐 Online. Turn voices, music and other sounds off with the 🔊 button.
  • Your location: nobody sees where you are in the game unless you share it. Stop any time in Phone → Bay Location.

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to access, correct or erase your personal data, and to raise a grievance with us. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

Children

India Bay is a cartoon game for a general audience, but online play lets you chat with strangers. Players under 18 must confirm that a parent or guardian agrees before they play online. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us information without your consent, contact us and we'll delete it.

Keeping it safe

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and database rules let each player read and change only their own save. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't share personal details in chat.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also announce it on the game's start screen.

Advertising

Billboards in the game show either India Bay's own ads for places in the game, or ads from businesses we work with. Advertisers choose cities, dates and age groups, and we may also choose ads by the look players picked (male or female); advertisers never receive any player's personal details, only how many times their ad was seen and tapped. Players under 18 only see child-safe categories such as food, education, sports, games and public service messages, and we never show ads for betting, real-money gaming, alcohol, tobacco, loans, crypto or dating. Tapping a sponsored billboard opens the advertiser's website in a new tab. To advertise, write to support@indiabaygame.com.
